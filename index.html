Free Online OCR

Extract text from images and scanned documents. Everything runs locally in your browser via WebAssembly - nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Drop an image here, or click to choose a file
JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP - screenshots, scans, and photos of text all work

How this works: this page loads the open-source Tesseract OCR engine compiled to WebAssembly and runs it directly in your browser tab. Your image file is read locally by JavaScript and is never sent to any server - you can disconnect your internet after the page and OCR engine have loaded and it will still work.
